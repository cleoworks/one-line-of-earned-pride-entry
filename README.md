One line, 1 to 240 characters, that gives the judge a quiet, earned moment of pride for someone in a scene. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's "One Line of Earned Pride" bounty, $5 in USDC.

The line (119 characters):

> After forty years of cutting stone for other men's houses, he laid the last brick of his own, and the door closed true.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

It writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (no trailing newline).
